Meet SoundTawn

SoundTawn, born Igwe Daniel Tochukwu, is an Afrobeat, Afropop and Afrofusion artist from Ebonyi State, Nigeria.

He began his musical journey in 2018, inspired by the happiness he feels when creating sounds that make people happy.

Through SoundTawn Music, he brings together African rhythms, contemporary sounds and personal expression to create music that connects with listeners and brings positive energy.
